## 1. Giant Spider

### Top pick
- **Source page:** https://freesvg.org/spider-vector-clip-art
- **Download URL:** https://freesvg.org/download/1291 (button requires an
  active session cookie from visiting the page first — a bare `curl` with no
  cookie/referer returns an empty response; a normal browser visit works fine.
  Direct raster fallback: https://freesvg.org/img/1311386118.png)
- **License:** CC0 / Public Domain (page's schema.org metadata:
  `https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/`; attributed to OpenClipart)
- **Pose/readability:** Clean side-view spider (like a tarantula/wolf spider),
  legs splayed to front and back so all legs read as distinct, fangs and
  cephalothorax/abdomen clearly separated. Currently colored (grey/black/tan),
  not a flat silhouette, but the outline is simple and well-suited to tracing
  into solid black. Best match for "side view, legs distinct, reads
  creepy/threatening" of everything found.
